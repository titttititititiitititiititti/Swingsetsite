Scattered across inboxes, spreadsheets, and someone's memory.

That's where most crews' paperwork lives today. A licence in an email from March. A medical on somebody's desktop. A roster called FINAL_v7. Swingset puts every document, date and signature for every worker in one live record — and does the chasing for you.

It knows before the gate does.

Every expiry, every missing ticket and every unsigned form, in the order it needs doing — before somebody gets turned away on a Monday morning.

  • One list for the whole crew. Urgent first, then what's coming up in the next 60 days.
  • Notify in one tap. The email to the worker is written for you — check it and press send.
  • Not now? Backburner it. It stays on the list and comes back when it matters.

It reads every document.

Licences, tickets, medicals, inductions, resumes. Upload a photo or a PDF and the details are pulled out for you — name, number, class, date of birth, expiry.

  • Dates you can trust. Anything hard to read is shown beside the document for a person to check.
  • Nothing changes until you tick it. The app proposes; somebody confirms.
  • Old copies are kept, not lost. A renewal replaces the old one and the history stays on file.

Got a whole folder? Drop it in.

Bringing a new client's paperwork across used to be a week of uploads. Now it's one. Select every file at once — tickets, licences, medicals, inductions, for as many people as you like.

  • It works out whose each file is, from the name on the document, not the filename.
  • You see the list first — "47 people in 412 documents" — before anybody is created.

Ask it anything.

In plain English, answered straight from your own records — not guessed. "Who can I send to Mount Brayling on Monday?" "Whose tickets run out in the next 60 days?"

  • Need it as a spreadsheet? Done. Every worker's phone number, every expiry, ready to download.
  • Fills in your forms. Attach a client's form, say who it's for, and get a filled copy back with every value sourced.
  • Shows its working. Every answer lists what it checked, so it can be checked.

Renewals on autopilot.

Switch it on once. Each worker gets a heads-up 60, 30, 14 and 7 days before anything of theirs runs out — and you get a summary of who's been told.

  • You see exactly who it will email before you turn it on.
  • Announcements to everyone with an account, marked so nobody mistakes them for their own paperwork.

Timesheets, signed on site.

Filled in from the roster, so the hours are already there. The worker signs on their phone, the supervisor gets it on the spot, and the signed PDF goes straight to accounts.

  • One swing, one timesheet — seven days from the day the swing starts.
  • Wrong day? Send it back with a note: "you took Thursday off, remember?"
  • Everyone gets a copy — download, open or share the signed PDF.

Every worker. One live file.

Documents on file, confirmed details, timesheets and a timeline of everything that's happened — open it on a phone at the gate and the PDF of their medical is one tap away.

  • Every change on the record — who did it, and when.
  • Resumes in your house style and submission emails, built from what's on file.
